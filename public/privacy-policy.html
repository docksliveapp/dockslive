Privacy Policy for Docks (Pvt.) Ltd

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Application: DPL Port / Docks (Pvt.) Ltd (Package: com.dockspvtltd.app)

1. Introduction

Docks (Pvt.) Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") operates the DPL Port logistics and customs clearance portal. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, business email, phone number, company name, and role credentials required for authenticating staff, clients, and verified transport carriers.
  • Logistics & Consignment Records: Bill of lading numbers, container numbers, goods declarations, customs clearance status, and tracking coordinates.
  • Financial Data: Invoices, receivables, and payables associated with business operational accounts.
  • Device Information: Technical device identifiers, push notification tokens, and operating system data strictly used for app functionality and security.

3. How We Use Information

  • To process shipping, transit, and customs clearance dossiers.
  • To authenticate users and enforce role-based access control (Admin, Client, Transporter, Staff).
  • To send operational push notifications (status updates, gate pass alerts, approval requests).
  • To maintain regulatory compliance with customs authorities and international maritime logistics standards.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is securely transmitted over HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption and stored using Google Cloud Firebase services with strict role-based firestore security rules. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or operational business data to any third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Our application uses Google Cloud Firebase for authentication and database services. These services adhere to Google Cloud's rigorous international privacy and compliance standards.
